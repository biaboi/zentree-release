What is Zentree?

Zentree is a thinking workspace where ideas grow as trees.

It is built for problems too complex for one chat window.

Instead of only storing answers, Zentree helps you organize:

branching thoughts
multiple paths
unfinished ideas
long-term reasoning
evolving decisions

AI can generate structures and options.
You keep control of the final shape.

How is Zentree different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is great for quick answers.

Zentree is built for deeper thinking.

ChatGPT gives linear conversations.
Zentree keeps reasoning visible as a tree.

ChatGPT responses fade over time.
Zentree structures can be refined and reused.

ChatGPT gives answers.
Zentree helps build thinking systems.

How is Zentree different from note apps?

Note apps store information.

Zentree develops ideas.

Notes are document-based and static.

Zentree is node-based and flexible.

Ideas can split, merge, expand, and evolve.

Who is Zentree for?

People who:

think in multiple directions
compare options deeply
work on long-term projects
want AI help without losing judgment

Core Philosophy

AI assists the thinking process.

It should help structure ideas, not own them.
